# Disha pilot runbook

The owner records real outcomes in Community & accounts → Pilot. Eleven checks begin as **not run**. A configured provider is not an acceptance pass. Mark a blocked check honestly and record what is missing. The downloadable JSON report retains timestamps and outcomes; these are owner-reported results, not independent certification.

## Preparation

Use test learner information and consenting participants. Grant only intended site access; invite separate learner, teacher, mentor and employer accounts using the matching emails. Keep the current site audience until the owner explicitly chooses a different audience. Obtain a physical pilot phone and an authorized opportunity export. The merchant supplies its own Razorpay test credentials and plan through the hosting secret store; AI configuration also belongs there. INTEGRATIONS.md gives exact variable names.

## Suggested 90-minute session

| Time | Activity | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15 min | Join accounts, check each role and revoked access | Roles exercised; forbidden access and invitation-reuse outcomes |
| 15–30 min | Discover a career, read a regional-language lesson, try voice and typed fallback | Language, device/browser and observed accessibility issues |
| 30–45 min | Prepare offline, disconnect, cold-reload, complete a lesson and reconnect | Phone model/browser, queued work and recovered progress |
| 45–60 min | Mentor confirms a session; employer receives and reviews a consented application | Participant consent confirmed; receipt/reference without private details |
| 60–75 min | Merchant sandbox purchase, receipt reconciliation and refund | Test reference, amount/status, unlock and refund revocation; never card details |
| 75–90 min | Verify opportunity facts and review AI answers/report | Provider source/check date; citation quality and unresolved issues |

Subscription renewal/failure/cancellation needs its own provider test cycle; do not mark it passed merely because a checkout was created. Verify callbacks can reach the private site or document the approved manual reconciliation process. Merchant policies and support must be approved by the merchant before any live charge.

## Payment checks

For a one-time test purchase, compare the provider-paid timestamp with the pass expiry. Repeat Check payment: expiry must stay fixed. Refund through the merchant's provider dashboard, then reconcile: a full refund removes the receipt's access and a partial refund suspends it for review. A stale paid event must not restore it. Another independent paid receipt must remain valid.

For subscriptions, verify no access before a paid active period. Confirm renewal updates expiry only after provider confirmation. Cancellation must not create access; previously verified paid access ends at its verified expiry. Subscription refunds and chargebacks remain outside automated handling and require merchant follow-up.

## Closeout

Record failed/blocked gates with concrete observations, assign follow-up work outside the application and download the report. Do not put secrets, contact information, household data or personal documents into evidence notes. Revoke temporary memberships and clear shared devices through the application's normal controls. No real participant invitation or payment is performed by this runbook itself.
